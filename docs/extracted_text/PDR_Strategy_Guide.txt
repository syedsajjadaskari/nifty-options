PDR STRATEGY 
Previous Day Resistance – Gap-Up Support Retest 
1. Strategy at a Glance 
Item Rule 
Concept Yesterday’s resistance zone is expected to act as support after a gap-up 
open. 
Bias Buy (long) 
Chart 5-minute candles 
Zone Small box from candle body to upper wick at the previous day’s 
high/reversal 
Next-day condition Market must open gap up 
Trigger Price returns to the zone, then a green candle forms 
Target 70 points (fixed) 
Stop-loss 35 or 40 points, depending on the situation 
2. Strategy Overview 
PDR stands for Previous Day Resistance. The strategy uses a resistance zone from the previous 
trading day to find a buying opportunity on the next day. 
When the market opens gap up and then pulls back into the marked zone, the zone is watched to 
see whether it now works as support. If a green candle forms after the zone is tested, the trade is 
taken. 
Sequence: Previous day’s high and reversal → Mark zone → Gap-up open → Price returns to zone → 
Support test → Green candle → Entry. 
3. Marking the Resistance Zone 
3.1 Identify the high 
1. Open the chart and review the previous trading day. 
2. Find the high where price reached a peak and then reversed. 
3. Locate the candle associated with that high. 
4. Note the candle’s body and upper wick. 
 

--- PAGE 2 ---
 
3.2 Draw the zone 
1. Upper boundary: the highest point of the upper wick. 
2. Lower boundary: the relevant candle-body level. 
3. Draw a small rectangle between the two boundaries. 
4. Extend the rectangle to the right so it stays visible on the next trading day. 
The zone is an area, not a single exact price. Mark only the actual resistance shown by price action, 
not the whole surrounding move. 
4. Next-Day Conditions and Entry 
4.1 Gap-up open 
The market must open gap up. Do not enter at the open. Wait for price to come back toward the 
marked zone. If price keeps rising without returning to the zone, there is no setup. 
4.2 Valid zone interaction 
Interaction Meaning 
Wick touches zone The candle’s wick reaches the marked area 
Body touches zone The candle’s body reaches the marked area 
Price enters zone Price moves inside the rectangle 
Very close approach Price comes close enough to count as a test of the zone 
Price does not need to hit one exact level. A move that stays far from the zone does not qualify. 
4.3 Entry 
After the zone has been tested, wait for a green candle and take the long entry on that confirmation. 
5. Target and Stop-Loss 
Parameter Value Note 
Target 70 points Fixed for every PDR trade 
Stop-loss (option 1) 35 points Used depending on the 
situation 
Stop-loss (option 2) 40 points Used depending on the 
situation 
At a 35-point stop the reward-to-risk ratio is 2.0 : 1; at a 40-point stop it is 1.75 : 1. Because the 
target is fixed, the stop-loss is the only variable, so choose between 35 and 40 before entering and 
do not move it after entry.

6. Execution Checklist 
• Previous day: clear high and reversal identified 
• Zone drawn from candle body to upper wick and extended forward 
• Next day opens gap up 
• Price returned to the zone (wick, body, inside, or very close) 
• Green candle formed after the interaction 
• Entry taken; target 70 points; stop-loss 35 or 40 points
