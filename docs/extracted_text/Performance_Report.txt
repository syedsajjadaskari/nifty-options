[P1] System Trading Strategy Performance
[P2] This report presents a trade-by-trade quantitative review of the NIFTY intraday execution strategy, traded on the 5-minute chart, for the 12-month period from September 2025 to August 2026. All 189 logged trades in this window are included. Every trade is executed with 6 lots (390 quantity at 65 per lot): 3 lots are exited at Target 1, fixed at 27 points, and the remaining 3 lots at Target 2 (60 or 70 points, as recorded). Profit and loss is measured in NIFTY spot-chart points and evaluated against an assumed capital base of ₹1,00,000.
[P3] Capital Efficiency
[P4] Against the assumed capital base of ₹1,00,000, the strategy generated a net trading profit of ₹7,69,860 across 189 trades on 180 trading days — a return of 769.86%, or 7.70× the base capital. All 12 months closed positive. All figures are before brokerage, statutory charges and slippage; their effect is quantified in the Transaction Cost Sensitivity section.
[P5] Core Quantitative Highlights
[P6] Strategy Framework
[P7] Every position is split into two equal legs of 3 lots (195 quantity each). Leg 1 is booked at Target 1, fixed at 27 points. Leg 2 is held for Target 2; once Target 1 is booked, the stop on Leg 2 moves to the entry price. Each trade therefore resolves into one of three outcomes:
[P9] Calculation Basis
[P10] • Scope: all 189 trades dated 1 September 2025 to 31 August 2026.
[P11] • P&L: point distance on the NIFTY spot chart × quantity. Target = 27 pts × 195 + Target 2 pts × 195; BE = 27 pts × 195; SL = stop distance × 390.
[P12] • Sequencing: trades are ordered by date and entry time for the equity curve, drawdown and streak calculations.
[P13] • Definitions: profit factor = gross profit (Target + BE) ÷ gross loss (SL); win rate (excl. BE) = Target ÷ (Target + SL); drawdown is measured on cumulative P&L after each trade.
[P14] • Costs & capital: results exclude brokerage, taxes and slippage (see Transaction Cost Sensitivity); the ₹1,00,000 capital base is an assumption used for return ratios.
[P15] Performance Overview
[P16] Core Quantitative Highlights
[P17] Outcome Distribution
[P18] Win rate methodology: Excluding breakeven trades, the win rate is 55.17% (80 Target / 145 decisive trades). Counting BE trades, each of which books Target 1, as positive outcomes, 65.61% of all trades (124 / 189) closed in profit.
[P19] Contribution of Target 1: The fixed 27-point partial exit converts every BE trade into a ₹5,265 gain. Across 44 BE trades this added ₹2,31,660, equal to 30.09% of total net P&L.
[P21] Equity Curve & Drawdown Analysis
[P22] Cumulative P&L is tracked after every trade. The curve’s lowest point was –₹15,990 on 15 Sep 2025 (trade 9); it turned positive on the next trade and did not fall below zero again. Each of the five deepest drawdowns was recovered to a new equity high within 17 trades of its prior peak.

[P43] Sample Size & Reliability
[P44] ORB (88 trades) and IRF (72 trades) form 84.7% of the sample and provide the most reliable statistics; IRF was profitable in 10 of 12 months and ORB in 9 of 12. RF (17 trades) is a moderate sample with a profit factor of 1.14. PDR (9) and PDS (3) recorded no stop-losses, but their samples are too small to treat a 100% win rate as representative. For the strategy as a whole, the 95% confidence interval on the win rate (excl. BE) is approximately 47.1%–63.3% (n = 145); its lower bound remains above the 42.75% breakeven requirement.

[P49] Conclusion
[P50] Over September 2025 – August 2026, the NIFTY 6-lot execution model generated ₹7,69,860 across 189 trades, with a profit factor of 1.93, a win rate of 55.17% excluding BE trades, and a profit in every month. Drawdowns were contained and recovered quickly, the deepest being ₹68,055 in June 2026. IRF and ORB carry the strategy, PDR and PDS add high-quality but infrequent trades, and RF is the main candidate for refinement. The edge remains intact under every cost scenario tested, with a profit factor of 1.52 even at ₹1,500 per trade.
