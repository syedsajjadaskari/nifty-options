## Findings (severity, location, evidence, failing input, fix)
No CRITICAL or MAJOR defect found in the controller, the exit model or the eligibility layer. Findings are MINOR/INFO.

**R5-01 MINOR - exit_policy.py:243-244 and 289/300 (`stop_fill` used for a stop that was not armed at the bar's open).**
When Target 1 and the post-T1 stop are touched in the SAME bar (stop2 only becomes active once T1 is booked inside that bar), the Leg-2 fill is `min(stop2, bar.open)`. If the bar OPENED below stop2 (before T1 was booked) the code fills Leg 2 at the open and logs "adverse slippage", although no gap through an active stop happened.
Input: LONG RF, entry 25000, bars [b0 (25000,25001,24999,25000), b1 (24990,25030,24988,25028)]. Result: T1_THEN_BUFFER_STOP, leg2_exit 24990, adverse_slippage_points 4.0, pnl 3315 (a stop-level fill at 24994 gives 4095; 4 points x 195 = 780 worse and a spurious slippage entry). Direction is conservative (never favourable) but the log is wrong and the spec says gaps THROUGH the stop. Fix: in the "T1 and stop2 in the same bar" branch fill at `stop2` (or `min(stop2, bar.open)` only when the open is already beyond stop2 AND T1 was booked before this bar); add a test.

**R5-02 MINOR - trading_day.py:229-237 (approved TieBreakPolicy picks arbitrarily inside one strategy).** `hit[0]` is the first record of the winning strategy in (strategy, method) sort order. If that strategy has records of OPPOSITE directions at the timestamp, the "approved policy" silently chooses one by method-name order. Input: `DayRules(tie_break=TieBreakPolicy(("ORB",), True))`, ORB "Method 1" LONG + ORB "Gap/200DMA" SHORT at 09:20 -> trades the SHORT. Latent today (no approved policy; same-strategy opposite directions never occurred in the data) but it contradicts "do not choose arbitrarily". Fix: after picking the priority strategy, require a single direction among its candidates, else treat as the unresolved conflict. Add a test.

**R5-03 MINOR (test gap) - tests/test_phase23_day_controller.py.** Surviving mutants (section "Mutation testing"): (a) legacy BREAKEVEN is not exercised through the controller (mutant removing "BREAKEVEN" from the DAY_FINISHED branch survives; the doc says it finishes the day); (b) no test sends a signal WHILE the second trade is active (mutant restricting the POSITION_ACTIVE branch to FIRST_TRADE_ACTIVE survives; behaviour is currently correct and only the defensive max-entries guard would mask a third entry); (c) TradeRecord.entry_price is never asserted. Fix: three small tests.

**R5-04 MINOR (documentation overclaim) - docs/phase2/phase3_readiness.md ("100+ new tests").** Test count went 318 (Phase 2.2 snapshot) -> 360: +42 net; test_phase23_day_controller.py holds 38 test functions / 42 collected cases. Reword ("42 new tests plus ~12 edited").

**R5-05 MINOR (documentation staleness) - execution_rules.md:16,17,40,54,63,66 and phase22_execution_policy.md:6,19,22.** Both still state "After Target 1 Leg 2 stop -> entry (breakeven) ... verified", with no SUPERSEDED banner (author_decisions_required.md has one for Phase 2.2). **R5-06 MINOR (registry consistency).** SEL-FIRST-001 says it supersedes EXE-SEL-001, but EXE-SEL-001 has superseded_by="" and implementation_status unchanged (only a text note); the supersession table in phase23_user_approved_rules.md shows "new: APPROVED". **R5-07 INFO - trading_day.py:96,217.** The day's final_state stays SECOND_TRADE_ELIGIBLE when no later PDR/PDS signal exists (61 of 188 trading days on the real data) and is not moved to DAY_FINISHED at the cutoff/end of session. **R5-08 INFO - run_trading_day input hygiene.** `records` of another day are not rejected up front (a 2026-01-13 PDR signal on a 2026-01-12 call is logged as ENTRY_NOT_FILLABLE, not as a day mismatch); naive signal timestamps with tz-aware bars raise TypeError (all-naive and all-aware both work, naive is read as IST). `day` is only a label.
