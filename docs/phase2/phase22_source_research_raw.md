Findings for PDR zone selection (supported by Ex 1-4, all consistent): (1) the candle used is the one carrying the day's high/reversal (Ex 1: 15:00 session high; Ex 2: 15:05 session high; Ex 4: 14:40 session high), but Ex 3 uses a late-session cluster/shelf of equal highs (~25,084-25,085) and skips the single-bar spike to 25,095.70 at 14:50 (that spike candle O 25,057.10 H 25,095.70 C 25,077.25: body 25,057.10-25,077.25 -> NOT used). (2) Zone TOP = upper wick tip (+0..+5 pt overshoot in Ex 1); zone BOTTOM = body top (the max(open,close)) of that candle (Ex 4 exact; Ex 2 ~+2pt; Ex 1 ~ +/-1). Not body bottom, not the low. (3) The picked highs are all in the LAST 1-2 hours of the previous session (Ex 1: 15:00, Ex 2: 15:05, Ex 3: 13:30-15:25, Ex 4: 14:40).

(Zone left edges in the drawings are arbitrary - not informative. The 14:50 spike candle on 2025-10-06 is O 25,057.10 H 25,095.70 L 25,054.60 C 25,077.25.)

Whether PDS is a mirror of PDR: concept yes (role reversal, gap in the opposite direction, zone from previous day's extreme candle, 'touch or very close' list, target 70, stop 35/40), but differences: (i) PDS entry needs a quality-qualified red candle / pin bar / small-support break; PDR just says 'a green candle' (no quality wording, PDR p2 s4.3); (ii) PDS states the 35/40 selection rule (distance of recent green candle) while PDR gives none; (iii) PDS zone wording 'body and lower wick' (p2/p3) vs PDR 'upper boundary = highest point of upper wick, lower boundary = relevant candle-body level' (p2); (iv) PDS has the explicit 'not a breakdown' and 'do not take a weak red candle' rules.
