## Method 1: original logic

At 09:20, evaluate the completed opening candle only (pp.1-3, p.11 Entry row). Mark the previous-day top for resistance, bottom for support (p.4 Major/Inner table). Long: established uptrend, price above 200 EMA with supporting bullish structure; first candle breaks and closes above that resistance, strong green body and limited upper rejection (p.3 steps 1-4). Short: downtrend, price below EMA with bearish structure; first candle breaks and closes below previous-day support, strong red body and limited lower rejection (p.4 steps 1-4). Candle rejection rules also allow suitably positioned conditional pins across methods (pp.9-10); long colour is subsequently clarified by handwriting.

No separate second confirming candle is specified. Break-and-close is the confirmation. No open-below-resistance requirement, minimum breakout distance, gap requirement or trendline requirement is written for Method 1. An open already beyond the level is not expressly disqualified. SL is fixed from entry, not candle low/high (pp.3-4 step 5; p.11 parameter table).

Failure to close beyond, lack of established trend, poor confirmation/rejection wick, or failure to wait for completion excludes the setup (pp.2-4, pp.9-10). The engine's use of wick extremes for previous-day top/bottom fits p.4 better than substituting an arbitrary multiday zone, although the real screenshots leave the zone boundary convention unsettled.

## Method 2: original logic

The inner level is a smaller resistance/support **inside the previous day's range**: a minor swing high or consolidation top (p.4 Method 2 introduction; support mirror p.6). It is not the previous-day major extreme. It is marked horizontally in pp.5-6 schematics. Relevant rising lows/falling highs establish a supportive trendline (p.5 steps 1-4; p.6 mirror; p.7 table). The first candle must break and close beyond the inner level at 09:20, with directional strength and limited adverse wick (p.5 steps 5-6; pp.6-7). A near-average breakout requires a convincing close beyond the EMA (p.5 step 7; p.7 table). Entry is 09:20; SL 30, target 60 (p.5 step 8; p.7 parameters; p.11 entry row).

The author's illustrations admit an inner breakout while the major extreme is unbroken. Requiring PDH/PDL for **every** ORB would contradict pp.4-7. The existing engine does **not** make that mistake: Method 2 has its own level.

Unspecified: exact establishing candles, count/lookback of sessions, swing confirmation width, tie handling, which swing to select, zone versus single boundary, whether bodies/wicks define the band, anchor choice, whether lines may pass through intervening candles, proximity to trendline, and allowable slope/distance. Prior sessions are visibly present in real reference charts, but a quantitative multi-session construction cannot be inferred from those drawings alone. P.7 explicitly labels its inner line approximate. No claim that the 13 handwritten dates are that exact setup can be proved without a drawn level.

## Gap setup: original logic and conflict

Gap up/down is relative to previous close (p.8 step 1; p.9 mirror). Trend can previously have been opposite (p.7 Special Setup; p.12 matrix). A 200 moving average is crossed and the **completed first candle must close beyond it** (p.8 steps 2-3; p.9). Strong directional body or suitable pin plus rejection/extension checks are required (p.8 step 4; pp.9-10). Entry is 09:20 after completion; not intrabar crossing and not an unspecified later candle (p.11 Entry row).

There are two competing readings: (a) overnight gap carries the **open** beyond the average; (b) opening gap plus first candle carries the **close** beyond. Reading (a) follows part of the wording; reading (b) is drawn on both pp.8-9 and supported visually by p.12 lower chart. Frozen code admits (a) only. Diagnostic gap-candle reconstruction retains a directional gap and prior close on the old side while also permitting first-candle traversal. It is source-supported, but cannot be promoted to the unique intended rule without resolving the wording/diagram conflict.

The PDF alternates DMA/EMA/MA. Literal 200-day SMA is distinct from EMA200 of five-minute closes; neither can be substituted silently. Handwritten p.3 labels **IRF's** average 200 EMA, 5 min TF, which is corroboration of the author's chart practice, not an unambiguous ORB indicator specification. The first-candle pin colour is not written explicitly in the original conditional row; handwritten p.5 20251127-1 explicitly requires green closing for ORB longs. Source p.1 symmetry supports a red short mirror, separately identified as inference.
