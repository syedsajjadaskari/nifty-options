Start with the five priority cases, then apply the same answers to related dates. Do not ask fifty copies of the same question. The author must still mark each claimed entry where direction/level is missing.

Coverage below counts dates with a reached engine rejection in that family, plus dates where it is the sole failed gate on at least one diagnostic path. These sets overlap, and author method/direction are unknown; they are not a promise that an answer will validate that many trades. Q1 is foundational despite only two sole-inner-gate cases. Q2 and Q3 have 22 and 8 sole-gate cases respectively. Q6 records missing evidence for all cases. No profit or journal-agreement optimization was used.

## Interview order

1. Have the author draw the intended entry, level and method on each priority chart, without showing candidate outcome claims.
2. Ask Q1 and Q6 first to identify what setup is being discussed; then Q2/Q3 for mechanical definitions and Q4/Q5 for indicator/trend interpretation.
3. Record exceptions and rejection conditions; apply a proposed general definition to all affected charts for review, not automatic activation.
4. Obtain separate source-evidence and engineering approval. Leave disputed answers unresolved rather than choosing the rule that matches more journal dates.
