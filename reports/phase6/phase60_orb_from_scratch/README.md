# Phase 6.0 - NIFTY 9:20 ORB built from scratch

**Written for:** the strategy owner. **Research only**: synthetic NIFTY index-point P&L x a declared 75 units (the index is not tradable; this is not CE/PE option P&L); no orders; ORB V2 is NOT integrated into the five-strategy portfolio; nothing was committed. No configuration is selected or ranked; neither historical window is untouched out-of-sample.

## What was built

A clean-room engine (`src/nifty_trading/research/orb_v2/`) from `Nifty_920_ORB_Rulebook (2) (1).docx`, a second independent implementation used only to audit it, 4 research configurations + 1 diagnostic, a standalone 30/60 backtest on 2025-09..2026-08 and 2022-09..2025-08 (5paisa; the secondary feed as a labelled sensitivity), reconciliation with the manual journal, 3 workbooks and 236 charts. See the file list at the end.

## Three findings that matter most

1. **The rulebook's screenshots come from the secondary-vendor feed, not 5paisa.** Its measured wick percentages (Example 2: 47% / 3%; Example 3: 66% / 27% / 7%) reproduce exactly on the secondary feed. The first 09:15 print differs between feeds (24 Sep: 25,142.55 vs 25,108.75), so the wick, pattern and gap tests are feed-dependent. With the secondary feed, Example 1 passes (BUY); on 5paisa it fails the pattern rule. Primary-window signals: 39 on 5paisa vs 31 on the secondary feed.
2. **The candle rules, not the level rule, decide the trades.** Of 247 sessions in the primary window, 230 pass the gap filter and 206 of those also pass the level test under the literal 'close beyond the level' wording (the level filter removes only 24; this is a consequence of the PROVISIONAL inner-level construction, which yields about eight levels per side - under a major-level-only reading it would remove far more); 120 also have a matching candle colour, 44 also match a pattern and 39 also pass the wick rule. The pattern definitions are the largest source of undefined behaviour: of the 39 V2-A trades in the primary window, 13 rest only on provisional pattern conventions (-120.0 pts) and 26 match the explicit full-body rule (+206.6 pts); ORB-V2-A is therefore a reference reading, not a purely written-rule result.
3. **The rulebook conditions are strongly consistent with the author's journal ORB dates (descriptive; not used to define any rule).** On the 85 evaluable manual ORB dates the opening candle's colour agrees with the EMA side on 100.0% of dates versus 37.0% of other sessions, a pattern matches on 49.4% vs 4.9%, and V2-A signals on 44.7% of them vs 0.6% of other sessions (5paisa). On the secondary feed the signal share on journal dates is 35.3%: neither feed is shown to be the author's for the journal. This is a relative-rate statement: V2-A still misses 47 of the 85 evaluable manual ORB dates. On the strict matches the journal's Target/SL labels agree with the V2 exit 29 times and disagree 0 times ('BE' has no 30/60 counterpart: 9); every journal time label is 09:15, so 'strict' means date + strategy.

## Answers to the 20 questions

**1. What does the latest rulebook require?** One decision at 09:20 on the completed 09:15-09:20 NIFTY 5-minute candle: |gap| within 250/300 points; close above (BUY) / below (SELL) the 5-minute EMA(200); the close beyond any one previous-day resistance/support (major or inner); green/red candle with at least one of hammer-or-shooting-star / engulfing / full-body and a wrong-side wick <= 20% of the range; entry at 09:20, stop 30, target 60, one trade, no re-entry. Details: `latest_rulebook_audit.md`.
**2. Completely defined:** instrument and timeframe, the 09:20 decision and no later entry/re-entry, EMA period and close-based direction (with no trade on equality), 'any one level is enough', candle colour, the wrong-side wick formula and 20% limit, the full-body rule, the zero-range rejection, the gap formula, stop 30 and target 60.
**3. Needs author approval:** the feed (OD-1), what counts as a break and whether it must cross the level (OD-2), how inner levels are found and what 'major' means (OD-3), hammer/shooting-star proportions and the engulfing reference at 09:20 (OD-4), the wick rule versus Examples 2 and 3 (OD-5), the gap limit (OD-6), entry price (OD-7), end-of-day time (OD-8), same-candle order (OD-9) - `unresolved_rule_definitions.md`.
**4. Built independently?** Yes: a new package that imports none of the old ORB code (AST-tested), reusing only loaders, the calendar, session classification and the bar-executability classifier; plus a second implementation (`audit.py`) with different methods that imports nothing from it. `orb_v2_architecture.md`.
**5. Does it reproduce 16 Sep (inner resistance)?** On the secondary feed: yes - BUY, EMA 25062.84, close 25106.75 beyond inner resistance zones while the previous-day high (25138.45) stays above, upper wick 11.0% (rulebook ~11%), full-body passes. On 5paisa it fails only the pattern requirement (total wick 28.3% > 20%). The author's two purple zones lie within ~2 points of engine swing zones, which is weak evidence only (many zones exist).
**6. Why do 17 and 24 Sep conflict with the wick rule?** 17 Sep: the real upper wick is 47.1% (secondary) / 43.4% (5paisa), a genuine violation of the 20% BUY limit, not a measurement error. 24 Sep: the real candle on the secondary feed has a 66.2% UPPER wick, which the written SELL rule treats as good (lower wick 7.3% passes), so the written rule signals it; the author's 'must not be taken' remark contradicts the rulebook table. On 5paisa the candle is a different shape (opening print) and passes via full-body. `original_examples_reconstruction.md`.
**7. Gap-ups entirely above the previous high recognised?** Yes under the literal rule (close beyond the major level); 62 gap-up and 46 gap-down sessions of that kind exist in the primary window and 8 V2-A trades come from them. Under the stricter crossing reading they are rejected (OD-2).
**8. Previous-day inner-resistance breakouts recognised?** Yes: 113 of the 221 level-passing sessions (before the gap filter) break only inner levels; all 39 V2-A trades in the primary window break at least one inner level, 20 of them only inner levels (the previous-day high stays intact) and 19 both. The inner construction itself is provisional.
**9. Fresh EMA-crossing setups recognised?** Yes (scenario B intrabar cross and C overnight-gap cross are eligible under the close-based filter): see `ema_gap_research.md` for counts by scenario and direction.
**10. Total ORB signals?** ORB-V2-A/B/C/D: primary window 39 / 39 / 37 / 34; 2022-25: 93 / 94 / 85 / 85 (5paisa). Diagnostic crossing-reading: 27 / 59.
**11. Executed trades?** Equal to the signals (every signal found an executable 09:20 fill; 0 unresolved exits in the primary window).
**12. Of the 50 previously missing ORB dates, how many now signal?** 16 under V2-A (16 under any of A-D). Attribution (overlapping): {"any": 16, "gap_beyond_prev_range": 1, "inner_only_level": 13, "ema_crossing_B_or_C": 5, "engulfing_only_route": 2}. The other 34 fail mainly the pattern and wrong-side-wick rules.
**13. Handwritten disputes reconciled?** V2 generates a signal on 4 of the 13 (2025-09-11, 11-28, 12-05, 12-24, all BUY and all stopped out at -30). 'Reconciled' cannot be asserted: the notes give no direction or level (OD-13). The other nine fail pattern / wick / level conditions (see `orb_13_handwritten_disputes.csv`).
**14. Of the 89 journal ORB rows, how many strictly match?** 38 (date + ORB + time label 09:15) under V2-A; 51 are manual-only (4 of them on non-session Saturdays); these are NOT exact trade matches (no direction/price in the journal). Verified references: 190 rows, 89 ORB rows/dates, 85 evaluable, 35 frozen ORB dates, 50 without a frozen signal, IRF on 34.
**15. On how many dates would ORB become eligible before the previous IRF entry?** 12 manual ORB dates (V2-A signals on 12 dates where the frozen IRF executed). V2 always fills at 09:20, before any IRF fill; the portfolio is unchanged.
**16. Standalone gross result?** V2-A primary: 39 trades, win rate 35.9%, PF 1.115, +86.55 pts, Rs +6,491. 2022-25: 93 trades, win rate 45.16%, PF 1.61, +921.00 pts, Rs +69,075. Other configurations are in `configuration_comparison.md` (no ranking).
**17. Costs and slippage?** V2-A primary with Rs 70/trade: Rs +3,761 at 0 slippage and Rs -2,089 at 1 point per side (PF 0.966); 2022-25 at Rs 70 + 1 point: Rs +48,615. Full grid: `trade_cost_sensitivity.csv`. Rs 70 is the owner's indicative figure, not a bill.
**18. 2022-2026 comparison:** A-2025: 13 trades, -210.0 pts, PF 0.364; A-2026: 26 trades, +296.6 pts, PF 1.7; B-2022: 12 trades, +133.6 pts, PF 1.742; B-2023: 32 trades, +189.1 pts, PF 1.345; B-2024: 28 trades, +148.3 pts, PF 1.29; B-2025: 21 trades, +450.0 pts, PF 2.667. Results are not stable year to year (Sep-Dec 2025 in the primary window is negative); the samples are small (12-32 trades a year) and in-sample for the earlier research.
**19. Remaining issues:** the feed question (OD-1); the undefined inner-level, break and pattern definitions; examples 2/3; the journal's missing fields; no option data; the DOCX could not be page-rendered; the 13 handwritten disputes were taken from the Phase 5.8 transcription (not re-read); the 5paisa/secondary difference is documented but only the opening print was investigated.
**20. Approvals needed before ORB V2 can replace the old ORB engine:** decisions OD-1 to OD-9 and OD-13 / OD-14 in `unresolved_rule_definitions.md`, then a frozen-baseline regression of the integrated portfolio.
